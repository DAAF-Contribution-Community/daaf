Synthetic Journal of Fixture Studies, Volume 7

Multipage Fixture
The first page of the combined fixture holds plain prose. Its planted sentence is: “The violet ferry
carried nineteen patient cartographers across the silver estuary.” That sentence appears on this page
and nowhere else.
The rest of the page describes the imaginary voyage. The cartographers drew the coastline twice, once
at low tide and once at high tide, and argued about which drawing should go into the final atlas. They
settled the matter by drawing a third map that showed both tides at once.
