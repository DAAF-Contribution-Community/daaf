Two-Column Fixture
The left column describes a small study of tidal
notebooks. The method relies on naturallanguage processing of short synthetic field notes
written by imaginary surveyors, and an experimental tagging pass that marks each note with a
weather label.
The notes were sorted by harbour, by week and by the
colour of the notebook cover. Each surveyor wrote in
pencil, and the pencil marks were transcribed by hand
before any counting began. The left column ends here.

The right column reports what the imaginary
surveyors found. Calm mornings were more common
than stormy ones, and the notebooks with green
covers held the longest entries. The right column
closes with a planted phrase: “the lantern keeper filed
every chart under a different constellation.”
Nothing in this column is real data; it exists so that the
two extractors can be compared on a page whose
reading order depends on column detection.
