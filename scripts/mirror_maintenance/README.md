# Mirror maintenance scripts (maintainer-only)

These scripts built, audited and documented the pinned Education Data Portal mirror (Portal vintage 0.26.1, Hugging Face revision `0ad00ce0e232c96b0642459e4e7326607a8d26aa`). Their logged runs date from 2026-07-21 to 2026-08-09, plus the 2026-10-28 runs of `54_generate-variable-dictionaries_b.py` (failed), `_c` (superseded) and `_d`. They are kept as the audit trail behind the education-data skills; the `variable-dictionary-*.md` headers, for example, name `54_generate-variable-dictionaries_d.py` as their generator. You do not need them to use DAAF.

- **Most of them read or write research workspaces that do not ship with DAAF** (maintainer-only workspaces, not shipped: `research/2026-07-21_FrameworkDev_EducationPortalSkills/`, `research/2026-08-06_FrameworkDev_MirrorV2Update/`; the override evidence strings in `54_generate-variable-dictionaries_b.py`, `_c` and `_d` also cite probe logs in `research/2026-09-18_AdHoc_Typesafe_Classifier_Experiments/probes/`). Every research path in the code and in the appended execution logs points into a maintainer-only workspace, whether it is written whole (`research/2026-...`) or assembled from parts (`BASE_DIR / "research" / "2026-08-06_FrameworkDev_MirrorV2Update"`, as 31 of the scripts do). In a fresh checkout those folders do not exist, so a script fails when it reaches one of those paths. Only 17 of the 88 scripts (10, 11, 14, 15, 25-34 and 36, with their revisions) probe the live Portal API or the public mirror without touching a workspace.
- **Most files carry an appended execution log and are immutable** (CLAUDE.md § Versioning & Naming). To re-run one, make a revision with `scripts/create_script_revision.sh` and point its input and output constants at your own workspace.
- **`snapshots/2026-08-07_endpoint_audit_vars/`** holds four Portal metadata pulls from 2026-08-07, the only inputs in this folder that ship. `41_variable-inventory_a.py` reads two of them (`endpoints_catalog.json`, `varlist_full.json`). It has not been run yet, and it still writes its outputs into the maintainer workspace.
- **`54_generate-variable-dictionaries_d.py` is maintainer-only as well.** It carries its 2026-10-28 execution log (`_b` failed one validation check and `_c` was superseded; both keep their logs; `_a` was never run). It reads its inputs from the maintainer workspace (the script-53 endpoint-to-file bridge and the script-41 variable inventory), writes the generated dictionaries there (`research/2026-08-06_FrameworkDev_MirrorV2Update/2026-10-28_dictionary-regeneration-overrides/generated/`), and its override table cites probe logs in another maintainer-only workspace. Regenerating the bridge and the `variable-dictionary-*.md` files at a new Portal vintage means working through the 41_a → 53 → 54_d chain from new revisions, made with `scripts/create_script_revision.sh`, of the logged 53 and 54_d and of the unlogged 41_a whose path constants point at a fresh maintainer workspace, not running these files as they stand: as they stand, they would overwrite the 2026-08-07, 2026-08-08 and 2026-10-28 outputs. The procedure, with the constants to change in each script, is in `.claude/skills/education-data-explorer/references/maintenance-live-api.md` § Regeneration procedure pointer.
- `38_boundary-years.py`, `38_boundary-years_a.py` and `51_laneB-value-slices.py` carry no execution log either. They are superseded attempts, replaced by `38_boundary-years_b.py` (its header records that the first two hung on the Portal API) and `51_laneB-value-slices_a.py`. They are not meant to be run.
