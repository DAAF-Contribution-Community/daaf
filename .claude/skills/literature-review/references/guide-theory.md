# Guide: Theory and Conceptual Papers

A formal-level guide for papers that contribute theory, a conceptual framework
or an argument rather than an empirical estimate. **This profile is DAAF's
design, not a published tool.** Theory papers are not graded on internal
validity: there is no result to be biased. The profile records how well the
argument is built, with quotes, and gives no rating. The researcher makes every
judgment.

## The five criteria

Record answers under guide `theory`. Answers: `Y` (yes), `P` (partly), `N` (no).

| Id | Criterion | Ask |
|---|---|---|
| `TH1` | Conceptual clarity | Are the key concepts defined, and used consistently? |
| `TH2` | Internal coherence | Do the propositions follow from the premises without contradiction? |
| `TH3` | Scope conditions stated | Does the paper say where, when and for whom the argument should hold? |
| `TH4` | Engagement with rival accounts | Does it address competing explanations and say why its account fits better? |
| `TH5` | Testable implications or empirical grounding | Does it say what evidence would support or undermine it, or rest on evidence it cites? |

The criteria follow DAAF's Literature Review design ("coherence, scope
conditions, engagement with rival accounts"), extended with conceptual clarity
and testability.

## What to quote

| Criterion | Quote |
|---|---|
| `TH1` | The definitions of the central concepts |
| `TH2` | The core propositions, in the paper's words |
| `TH3` | The sentence stating scope, or the passages that imply it |
| `TH4` | The passage engaging the strongest rival account |
| `TH5` | The stated implications or the evidence the argument rests on |

## How DAAF and the researcher use it

1. The reader drafts an answer and a quote per criterion.
2. Each answer backed by a quoted passage is an appraisal claim
   (`formal-review.md` § Recording checklist answers): guide `theory`, item
   `TH1` to `TH5`. A criterion the paper never addresses goes in the card's
   Appraisal prose with its reason.
3. The researcher reviews the profile and adds their own reading; the record
   quotes them. No overall rating is given.

At the standard level, the paper note for a theory paper uses these five
criteria as prompts and records the paper's design as `theory`, with no weight
on internal validity; its weight reflects how much the review's argument relies
on it.

## How theory papers enter a synthesis

They organize findings (a concept matrix, a mechanism a realist synthesis
tests), supply rival explanations to check, and define scope conditions. They
do not supply effect evidence: a finding about effects never rests on a theory
paper alone, and its certainty is judged from the empirical studies behind it.
